AI-assisted triage and clinical workflow for retinal care. The AI supports clinicians — it never replaces them.

HIGH risk MEDIUM risk LOW risk Decision point Direct specialist consult

How to read this diagram: A nurse registers the patient and captures a retinal image. The AI analyzes the image and produces a risk score, risk level (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW) and biomarker findings — used for triage and decision support only. Each care level has its own waiting room: HIGH and MEDIUM risk patients wait in the MBBS queue (HIGH cases are served first), while LOW risk patients go to the Nurse waiting room. If a specialist is available, HIGH risk patients route directly to them (in-person, Teams or WhatsApp) without an MBBS visit. The doctor opens the patient panel, reviews image, AI findings and previous visits, then either prescribes or refers to a specialist; the specialist reviews the case on the retinal reading screen, completes an assessment and signs off the prescription. Completed visits return to the Nurse discharge desk, then are archived to history. Administration monitors queues and staff to keep the waiting rooms moving.
